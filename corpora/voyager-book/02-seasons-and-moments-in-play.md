# Chapter Two: Seasons and Moments in Play

Chapter One fixed the shape. This chapter fixes the numbers the shape needs to be played, and no more of them than that.

## What a season costs

A season is a year, and a year belongs to the world it is lived on: an orbit is a fact, not a convention. Where no world states its own reckoning, the standard year holds, and a season of it ages a character by 1 year. Choosing how a season is spent is the player's, always. Nothing else about a season is drawn.

## What a moment risks

A moment is not resolved by dice read off a table; it is resolved by chance stated plainly, because chance is how the world itself works. Every moment carries a probability of going against you, written as the physicist writes it, and the referee who sets it for the situation must keep it between 0.05 and 0.95. Certainty is not on offer, in either direction. The engine draws the number; the referee never does. A moment leaves a record: its kind, the chance as it was stated, what was drawn, and what it did.

## What stage is

Stage is not written anywhere and never stored: it is counted, kind by kind, from the record of moments a character has faced. You have the stage your life adds up to, nothing more. A kind you have never faced shows its doors without their prices. A kind you have faced at least once shows them priced.

## Unsettled

Named here so the machinery raises them instead of guessing:

- What a season of preparation or pursuit yields, beyond the year it costs. The yield belongs to the chapter that gives characters capabilities, and it is not written yet.
- Whether the at-least-once threshold for an open moment stays, or grows into a graded eye as stage deepens.
